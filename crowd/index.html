The Faster-is-Slower Paradox: When an emergency alarm sounds, crowds push harder toward the door. Without an obstacle, mutual tangential friction wedges agents into a rigid arch that halts evacuation. An off-center column breaks bilateral symmetry, absorbs upstream crowd pressure, and allows smooth alternating entry into the doorway.
